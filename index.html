DeepBrain at Berkeley · Fall 2026

Computational neuroscience, neuro-AI and brain-computer interfaces.

We are a community of students into computational neuroscience: signal processing, neuro-AI and brain-computer interfaces. Small teams pick one concrete goal and spend the semester on it.

Who we are

What we do

  • Teach the concepts

    The ideas behind neurotechnology and computational neuroscience, explained from the ground up.

  • Learn by doing

    Through research and projects, each with a real dataset, device or study behind it.

  • Keep it open

    To every student, whatever their experience.
